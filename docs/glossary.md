# Agronomy Glossary

**APMC Mandi:** Agricultural Produce Market Committee. Wholesale markets in India where farmers sell their produce.

**Kharif Crop:** Monsoon crops sown in the rainy season (June-October) in India. Examples: Rice, Maize, Cotton.

**Rabi Crop:** Winter crops sown in October-November and harvested in spring. Examples: Wheat, Mustard.

**Quintal:** A unit of mass equal to 100 kilograms. The standard unit for measuring crop yield in India.

**NPK:** Nitrogen, Phosphorus, and Potassium — the three primary nutrients found in agricultural fertilizers.
